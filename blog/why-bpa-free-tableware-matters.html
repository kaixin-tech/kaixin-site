Why BPA-Free Tableware Matters for Your Customers' Health

"BPA-free" has become a label buyers trust — but what does it actually mean, and why should it matter to your customers? Here is the plain-language answer for brands selling dinnerware.

What Is BPA, and Where Is It?

BPA (Bisphenol A) is an industrial chemical used to make certain plastics and epoxy resins. It can be found in many conventional plastic plates, cups and food containers — especially when they are heated, scratched or old, BPA can leach into food and drinks.

Why Buyers Care

Health-conscious consumers — especially parents — actively look for "BPA-free" on products their families eat from. For a brand, selling BPA-free tableware is both an ethical position and a sales advantage. It reduces liability risk and aligns with global trends restricting hormone-disrupting chemicals in food contact materials.

How Rice Husk Tableware Avoids the Problem

Quality rice husk dinnerware contains no plastic and no BPA. It is made from natural rice husk fiber and a small food-grade starch binder — no petroleum-based resins. Our full range, from kids sets to coffee cups, is certified BPA-Free, Melamine-Free and food-grade.

  • ✓0 BPA, 0 melamine, 0 plastic in the material
  • ✓Safe for hot food and microwave reheating (3–5 min, do not microwave empty)
  • ✓Ideal for babies, kids and daily family use
  • ✓Verified by third-party labs (SGS / LFGB)

How to Verify a "BPA-Free" Claim

Don't take the label on faith. Ask suppliers for test reports from recognized labs. We keep ours public on the certifications page — including BPA-Free, LFGB, REACH and SGS — so your customers can trust the claim too.
