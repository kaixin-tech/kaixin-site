About Us

Turning Rice Husk Waste into Healthy Tableware

Shenyang Kaixin Technology Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer specializing in eco-friendly plant-fiber products, with a focus on sustainable living.

Who We Are

Founded in 2016, we operate a modern 5,000 m² facility in Shenyang, Liaoning, with a team of dedicated professionals. Our main products are high-quality rice husk dinnerwares that are BPA-free, biodegradable, and comply with international standards for food contact.

We provide comprehensive OEM and ODM services to clients worldwide — from custom colors and sizes to branded packaging.
