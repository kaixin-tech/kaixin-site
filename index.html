Sustainable Source

From the Rice Field, Not from Plastic

Our raw material is rice husk — a natural by-product of rice harvesting that used to be burned or discarded. We give this agricultural waste a second life as safe, beautiful, reusable tableware, turning farm residue into everyday products instead of air pollution.

  • ✓Plant-based: rice husk + food-grade starch binder
  • ✓Naturally BPA-free, plastic-free and biodegradable
  • ✓Repurposes farm waste into durable daily products
  • ✓A truly circular, low-carbon material choice

Our Factory

From Rice Husk to Finished Tableware

Raw rice husk is purified, blended with 20% food-grade starch as a natural binder, then molded under high temperature and high pressure — with no glue and no harmful chemical additives.

  • ✓5,000 m² modern facility with dedicated professionals
  • ✓Strict quality control, certified by SGS and other authorities
  • ✓Full OEM & ODM service: custom color, size and packaging
  • ✓Exported to Europe, America, Canada, Australia, SEA, Middle East
